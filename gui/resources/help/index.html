PathMux Dashcam Explorer

Trip manager, GPS extractor, and video builder for multi-channel dashcam footage.

PathMux scans dashcam footage directories, groups video segments into trips based on timestamp gaps, and caches the results as JSON manifests. Trips can be browsed in the GUI, exported as GPX or KML tracks, and assembled into per-camera MP4 files or a synchronized 4K collage.

Quick Start

  1. Run Tools > Setup Wizard to configure ffmpeg, ExifTool, and your camera profile.
  2. Use Manifests > Scan Source Directory to scan a dashcam footage folder.
  3. Click a manifest in the left panel to view its trips.
  4. Double-click a trip tile to open Trip Properties, build video, or export GPS.

GPS Cold-Start Warning

Important: Dashcam GPS receivers typically require 30–120 seconds to acquire a satellite fix after power-on. No GPS data is recorded during this period. If you use GPS track data for mileage logs, tax records, or legal documentation, be aware that the first portion of every trip may be missing from the GPS track. The distance reported by PathMux is the great-circle distance between the first and last recorded fix, not the total odometer distance.

Supported Cameras

PathMux uses camera profiles to support different storage layouts. The built-in profile targets the Pruveeo D90 360° four-channel dashcam (Front, Rear, Left, Right subdirectories; MPEG-TS container; LIGO GPS stream). Additional profiles for other cameras can be created interactively with pm_probe --wizard or via Tools > Setup Wizard.
